# The v8 strip (PR 1 of #151) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the surface's strip is redrawn as the owner approved it in mockup v8. That means a calm name button as the strip's top edge with one marks line under the name. A muted strip recedes, and each strip carries one colour. The RET mark and the clip lights are gone. A stereo L/R meter sits in the fader's track, with the dB scale on its right. A left column holds an empty LUFS slot and the group's name. A SEL key at the bottom left replaces ☰. There is no new data source: the LUFS readings come in PR 2.

**Architecture:**

- **Pure decisions first** (`.claude/rules/ui-rust.md`): whether a strip is narrow (`arrange`), how the marks line fills (`behave/marks.rs`) and which slot each meter bar reads (`behave/meter.rs`). Each is natively tested at its boundaries.
- **The glue carries them out:**
  - `MuteView` draws the name first, then the marks line.
  - `FaderView` draws the stereo bars in its own track when given meters.
  - `StripView` builds the new zone: the info column, the scale on the right, the fader, and the SEL key.
- **The stylesheets carry the mockup's sizes,** which are binding.
- **Nothing in the hub, the proto or the Live script changes.** `meter_source: lr` on the site is the main session's call after its PC re-test (#151 plan). It is not part of this PR.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (fohmixer-ui: Leptos 0.7, web-sys), CSS, Playwright (Chromium and the WebKit iPad project).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-lufs-design.md` §6.2–6.5 and §9's E2E list (the strip parts only). The surface is `docs/mockups/lufs-v8.html` (approved by the owner on 2026-10-26, #151). The mockup is **binding**: sizes, texts, states, the give-way rule, `MUTED` `#a8474f` 7 px. Read the mockup's CSS from line 812 (`NEW in v2`) to its end, and its JS functions `muteButton`, `zone`, `selButton`, `marks`, `headHeight`, `oneColour` and `recedes`. Where the spec's prose and v8 disagree, v8 wins (see "Rulings" below).

## Rulings on conflicts *(the plan's own, from the spec, v8 and the ticket)*

1. **Head size and colour edge.** The spec's §6.4 "Unchanged" bullet keeps the 30 px button and its corner marks, and §6.4/§0 say the colour edge is 4 px. v8's head paragraph (§6.4, "The head's layout") and the mockup make the button 37 px with a marks line under the name and a **3 px** edge (`.strip.v8 .strip-head > .mute.calm::after`). v8 applies.
2. **The zone's head.** §6.3 says "the head, 30 + 2 + 22 px … unchanged". v8's head is 37 + 2 + 20 with no top padding, the same 59 px. v8 applies.
3. **PR order.** §10 puts proto/hub/DSP in PR 1 and the surface in PR 2. The owner-approved plan on #151 (the DECIDED comment of 26.10, after v8) makes PR 1 the strip and PR 2 the LUFS source. The ticket applies, and Task 6 corrects §10.
4. **The `MUTED` word.** §6.4's muted bullet keeps v7's `--mute` at 7.5 px. v8 uses `#a8474f` at 7 px, 800, 0.5 px spacing (0.3 px under 78 px). v8 applies.
5. **"RET" in the instance tag.** Besides the `.strip-ret` mark (§6.4), the instance tag (`.strip-tag`, only in a group whose strips differ) writes `MASTER · RET` on a return. The owner ruled that RET "must not be written on a fader", so the tag shows the instance alone.
6. **Clip lights.** The spec removes the clip light from the strip (§6.3, "lose their strip use"). The owner's reason, that Live mixes in 32-bit float, holds for the channel detail's meter too. So `MeterView` loses its clip light everywhere, and `behave::peak::clips` / `CLIP_LEVEL` are deleted (MVP: no dead code).
7. **The tap hint's place.** The spec and v8 say nothing about where the hint goes once ☰ leaves the head. Today's hint covers the name button above ☰. Under the new key it is drawn **above the key** instead, one head-width wide: the finger covers the key, not the zone above it. Its text is `hold SEL` / `for detail`.
8. **Narrow is the strip's own width.** v8 uses `w < 78`. A wide strip (1.1×) is narrow by its own width, `1.1 × w < 78`, never by the page's base width.
9. **The group's name.** It takes the mockup's fixed place in the info column: top 40 px (44 px narrow), height 96 px, `--faint`. The fader law no longer places it, so `behave::scale::side_style` goes if nothing else uses it.

## Review Focus

1. **The narrow boundary.** A page whose strip width is exactly 78 px must not be narrow, and 77.99 px must be. A wide strip at a base width of 70.9 px (78.0 px of its own) must not be narrow, and at 70.8 px it must be. A mistake here wraps a mark out of the line or clips `MUTED` on the phones. Pinned in Task 2 (native) and Task 3 (the sweep sets both flags).
2. **All four marks on a muted narrow strip.** `!`, a muted chain of 12, a fader-link mark and `MUTED` at 64 px. The fader mark must yield, and `!`, the chain and `MUTED` must stay whole. Pinned in Task 3 (the sweep's full combination table at 64 and 83 px).
3. **The key at −∞.** A down at the key's spot while the cap rests at −∞ must hit the key, never the cap, and a down on the key must never move the fader (the cap's widest reach stays 3 px clear). Pinned in Task 5.
4. **A new layout that switches `meter_source`** between `level` and `lr` while the page is open. The strips rebuild their bars: with `level` both bars show the one value, with `lr` the two differ. There is no reload and no console error. Pinned in Task 4.
5. **A conflicted strip.** The key is inert and dimmed like the rest (`states.css`), and the marks line keeps `!`. A hold there opens nothing. Pinned in Task 5.
