# A plug-in's editor window on the surface (#71, D17, F28)

What the PC tests of 2026-10-09 established (details and evidence on #71). Read this before touching the Pro-Q 4 screen.

## Never in a running Live first

- **Anything that sends input to a plug-in window is proven against the plug-in in a separate host process before it runs in a running Live.** A posted drag into Pro-Q 4.02 inside the band instance was followed by `is_editor_open = false`, and Live crashed: `FabFilter Pro-Q 4.vst3`, `0xc0000409`, Exception Data 7 (`abort`). The lead is FabFilter's own fix of "a crash when closing the interface with a text editor open". Two quick presses on a value field read as a double-click and open that text field. The owner postponed the FabFilter update (#71), so the hub must never close an editor while a text field may be open.
- **The isolated host:** Carla 2.5.10 win64, its bridge alone, on the dummy engine. No audio driver is loaded; check that the process has no `asio`, `audioses`, `mmdevapi` or `wdmaud` module.
  - It lives in the PC's probe folder.
  - Command: `$env:CARLA_BRIDGE_DUMMY = '1'` (exactly `1`), then `Start-Process <…>\Carla\carla-bridge-native.exe -ArgumentList @('vst3', '"<…>\FabFilter\FabFilter Pro-Q 4.vst3"', '""') -PassThru`.
  - Use `Start-Process`, which detaches. `Process.Start` with handles inherited keeps the MCP shell waiting until it times out.
  - The label is `""`; `"(none)"` fails ("Failed to get plugin description").
  - The editor is a top-level `Pro-Q 4 (GUI)` of class `JUCE_…`.
  - Closing that window ends the bridge with `0xC0000005` whatever the plug-in's state, so this host cannot reproduce the close crash.
- **Every injection checks the target first:** the point's window (`WindowFromPoint` → `GetAncestor(GA_ROOT)` → owner PID) must belong to the host meant, never to Live by accident.

## What Live gives

- **Opening and closing:** `PluginDevice.is_editor_open` (get / set / observe, Live 12.4.3+) opens and closes a plug-in's editor. It reads on the PC's 12.4.6 through the FohMixer script's generic `get_prop` / `set_prop`, for a device on a track and one inside a rack chain. Two editors can be open at once in one instance.
- **Identity:** `class_display_name` is the plug-in's product name (`Pro-Q 4`). It holds even when the device is renamed; `name` is the device's name.
- **The window:** a top-level `Vst3PlugWindow` titled `<device>/<track>`. The title holds a track name, so never log or print it. Pro-Q's own UI is one child, `FF_UIWindow`, at (8, 31), 1349×809 at 100 %, in a 1365 × 848 window. Find the new window by comparing the window list before and after the open; Live does not make it the foreground window.
- **Its size (PR G):** FabFilter's VST3 editor resizes freely by its window. In Carla's bridge on the PC, `SetWindowPos` of the host window to 766 × 1300 gave a 760 × 1271 client and Pro-Q 4 laid itself out upright (a tall EQ display, the top and bottom bars narrowed); the old size back gave 1349 × 809 again. Whether Live's `Vst3PlugWindow` passes a resize on to Pro-Q the same way is for the PC check (below). The PC's screen is 2560 × 1440 (the work area less the taskbar). **The inert spot is known only at 1349 × 809** (`plugwin::KNOWN_SIZE`, where the PC verified it): at 760 px wide, 0.405 × the width lands on Pro-Q's Undo button, so the guard taps only at exactly that size (#74 review, below), whatever size the editor had at its take.
- **Parameters:** Live exposes only one Pro-Q 4 parameter (`Device On`) unless the owner configures them in Live, so the EQ is driven through the window, not the parameters.

## Capture

- `PrintWindow(hwnd, hdc, PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT)` gives the whole editor even behind other windows, in about 17 ms per 1365×848 frame. It renders on the window's thread, which is likely Live's, so it is the fallback only.
- **Windows Graphics Capture** (the `windows-capture` crate, MIT) reads the DWM surface instead.
  - On the PC's Windows 10 19044 its yellow border cannot be switched off (needs 20348).
  - A fully off-screen or minimised window gives no frames.
- JPEG q75 of a frame: about 5 ms and 62 KB (System.Drawing), so about 2 MB/s at 30 fps.

## Input

- **Posted mouse messages** to `FF_UIWindow` give hover (`WM_MOUSEMOVE`), clicks (`WM_LBUTTONDOWN` / `UP`) and the wheel (`WM_MOUSEWHEEL`, screen coordinates). They give **no drag**, because FabFilter reads the real cursor and button state.
- **A real drag is injected touch** (the owner's decision on #71):
  - Use `InitializeTouchInjection` / `InjectTouchInput`, or the synthetic-pointer API: DOWN `INRANGE|INCONTACT|DOWN`, UPDATE `INRANGE|INCONTACT|UPDATE` every ~16 ms (re-send the last point at least every 100 ms while the finger rests: Windows cancels a contact that gets no frame for 100 ms; the hub's window worker re-sends every 50 ms), and UP `UP` at exactly the last point.
  - **Never set `POINTER_FLAG_PRIMARY`**: every call then fails with `ERROR_INVALID_PARAMETER` (87).
  - `POINTER_TOUCH_INFO` is 144 bytes, `POINTER_INFO` 96.
  - The editor must be the window at that screen point.
  - The system cursor follows the contact; put it back with `SetCursorPos` after the gesture.
  - A real-mouse drag (`SendInput` / `mouse_event`) works too.
- **On Pro-Q 4:**
  - A single tap on the curve creates nothing; a double-click creates a band.
  - A touch drag that starts on a band moves it.
  - **A double tap on a band opens its frequency text field**: the close-crash state.

## The Pro-Q 4 screen (#71 PR E)

The surface's side (the cards and the screen) is in `.claude/rules/ui-rust.md`, its E2E in `.claude/rules/e2e.md`. The PC check (the plan's task 7) ran `eq-probe` against Carla's bridge, then the first open in the band Live (0.1.0-dev.48, at the end of this file); PR G's check followed on 0.1.0-dev.49 to .51.

- **The page's zoom (PR F) never reaches the hub:** two fingers zoom and pan the page's own view of the picture (1× to 4×, `behave::eq::View`, the approved mockup `docs/mockups/proq-zoom-v1.html`), and a finger maps through it, so `eq_input` stays in the picture's pixels and the PC's window never moves or scales; the other engineer never sees it. **The overview takes no edits** (PR I, the owner's ruling of 2026-10-11, #71 comment 6106055713): below `behave::eq::EDIT_ZOOM_MIN` (2×) a first finger never reaches the editor (`Spot::Overview`): a lift within the 6 px slop zooms to `TAP_ZOOM` (3×) with the tapped picture point in the area's middle, clamped as a pinch is (`Viewer::zoom_on`), and a drag does nothing; a hint over the whole picture says so. From 2× on, the first finger's down waits 120 ms or 6 px (a pinch sends the PC nothing; a second finger on a contact cancels it at its last point first), so a tap shorter than that is sent when it lifts: its down at once and its up on the first frame 40 ms later (PR G, `behave::eq::TAP_MS`; before, both went together and the worker injected them back to back, a contact of no time), and no down goes before that up (#74 review: a quick double tap waits for it), so the PC never gets a contact shorter than 40 ms from the page (the guard's own tap holds 30 ms). The router and the worker's 10 ms steps may still bring a down and its up closer at injection: the E2E checks their order only.
- **The device's shape (PR G, the owner's ask of 2026-10-10; the design comment on #71):** the editor takes the aspect of the page's picture area. `eq_open` carries `area {w, h}` (CSS px; absent: the editor keeps its size) and the page sends `eq_area {w, h}` when its area changed by a pixel or more and rested 300 ms while it holds an open editor (`behave::eq::AreaWatch`, the frame loop). The hub sizes the picture (`eq::size::editor_size`: the area's aspect at 1349 × 809's pixel count, scaled down into the room, each side at least the minimum; a room smaller than the minimum keeps the editor's size), posts the window's rectangle for it (the frame beyond the picture, measured at the take, added; the window kept where it stands when it fits in the work area there, else moved in on each side it would cross, `plugwin::inside`, #74 review; `SWP_ASYNCWINDOWPOS | SWP_NOZORDER | SWP_NOACTIVATE`), and reads the picture child's size back each worker step (`GetClientRect`, no message) until it lands within 2 px or 1 s passed. A resize asked while a finger of that session is down on the editor waits until the contact ended, the newest only (#74 review: Pro-Q never lays itself out under an injected contact). A resize that settles unlanded posts the window's rectangle before it back at once (`reverted` in its record): Live's frame may have taken the size and Pro-Q not (a clipped picture), or Live's thread may land both later, in order (the guard's placement lands after both, below). An open resizes right after the take and **before the open answers**, so the first frame is the new picture. The page sizes the picture from the frames (no `eq` message tells it); the hub's state clamps the points to the new size (each settled resize, and a grab of another size, `Sized`).
  - The room is the window's work area (`MonitorFromWindow`, `GetMonitorInfoW` `rcWork`) less the frame; the window moves into the work area when its new size does not fit where it stands (a window low on the screen goes up for an upright phone's picture), and the take's rectangle, its place and its size, is what the release puts back.
  - The minimum: the Windows backend asks no smaller than Pro-Q 4's own, `win::PRO_Q_MIN` = 760 × 335 (read on the PC, 0.1.0-dev.49: `eq-probe --min-probe` asked 160 × 120 and the editor took 760 × 335); an upright phone's picture is 760 wide, so its aspect bends (the page fits the picture with bands at its sides). The sim and the tests keep `plugwin::MIN_PICTURE` (600 × 400). A resize lands when the picture reads the size asked, or, after `RESIZE_MS`, at whatever size the editor took once it left its old one (`plugwin::settled`; on the PC, 0.1.0-dev.50, Live gave an upright phone's 760 × 1361 as 760 × 1275, and the hub had reverted it); a picture still at its old size never landed and is reverted. `eq-probe` stays strict (it reports a size the editor would not take).
  - **The quiet time (#74 review, round 3):** nothing is posted to an editor's window, no resize (one asked meanwhile waits, the newest only), no guard placement and no release's rectangle (#74's final review: the release posts none then, below), until `plugwin::QUIET_MS` (100 ms) after a contact of its session ended (an up, a cancel, a refused update's cancel, the guard's own up, another editor's guard cancelling it: `Editor::ended`, `plugwin::quiet`): Live's thread takes posted messages before injected input, so a move posted right after an injected up could lay Pro-Q out anew before the up lands, at the old screen point (a band's drag ending somewhere else).
  - **The close puts the editor at the known size first, in two placements each seen to land (#74 review, I1; round 2, N1/N2):** the guard (after its contact ended and the frames stopped; no resize reaches the editor from then on) waits for the quiet time and for a resize on its way to settle (an unlanded one posts its way back as it settles), then places the editor, whatever size it reads. R is the window's rectangle for 1349 × 809 (at the take's place, moved into the work area when it does not fit there, `plugwin::known_rect`). First R moved by 1 px is posted (`plugwin::aside`: inward, round 3: to the right when that still lies inside the work area's right edge, else to the left; the other way when the window stands exactly there, so the window cannot read it before it lands; that other way may lie one pixel past the work area's left or right edge, harmless: it only proves that what was posted before it landed, the known rectangle follows before the tap, and no input goes in between) and read back (`GetWindowRect`, no message) until the window is there: Live's thread takes its posted messages in order, so that read proves every rectangle posted before it landed (a resize, its way back). A read of the size alone cannot: a resize asking the size the picture already reads "lands" at its first look, and a resize and its way back may land between two looks, or change nothing when Live's window keeps its size. Then R is posted and read back until the window is at R and the picture (`GetClientRect`) reads exactly 1349 × 809 (`plugwin::Placement`, `plugwin::guard_step`). Only then the tap at (546, 15): Live takes posted messages before injected input, so a tap while a rectangle still waited would meet another layout (Undo, the EQ display). A placement not seen within 1 s of its own post fails the guard, which taps nothing (the editor stays open in Live: `left open in Live`; the `problem` says what the window read and what was posted, `plugwin::not_landed`). Live and the set keep the editor at 1349 × 809, the size it closed at (an editor taken at another size, its last one or one given on the PC, closes at the known size). The release of an editor Live keeps open (a failed guard or turn-off, the hub's stop) posts the take's rectangle again when it was changed (best effort); one Live closed gets nothing posted, and neither does one whose session's contact ended less than `QUIET_MS` ago, the release's own end of a contact still down included (#74's final review: Live's thread would land the rectangle before that injected up; `plugwin::repost` → `Repost::NotQuiet`, a warn line in the hub log): it stays where it is, open in Live.
- **Protocol** (`fohmixer-proto` `client.rs`, `eq.rs`):
  - The page sends `eq_list {binding}`, `eq_open {instance, path, area: {w, h}}` (PR G: the picture area in CSS px, optional), `eq_area {w, h}` (PR G), `eq_input {touch: down|move|up|cancel, x, y}` (the picture's pixels, floats), `eq_close` and `eq_frame_ack {session}` (PR I: a frame of its open editor arrived).
  - The hub answers `eq_list {binding, items: [{path, place, name, picture}], error}`, `eq_locks {items: [{instance, path, mine, since}]}` (to every page at each change, and at the hello) and `eq {instance, path, state: opening|open|closed, session, width, height, reason, since}` (to the holder).
  - `reason` is `locked` (that editor is another client's, with the holder's `since`), `in use` (another client holds another editor, with its `since`: one on the PC's screen at a time, round-3 ruling), `unknown` (not in a list this hub gave), `off`, `exit`, `detach`, `switch`, `window closed`, `closing`, `moved` (the open's read found another device or name at the listed path: list again), `open on the PC` (its editor is already open in Live), `unread`, and (round-2 review) the open's failures: `instance offline` and `Live did not answer` (an open's or a list's call through the script got no answer: `router::eq::live_reason` maps `LiveError::Offline` / `Timeout`; a refused request keeps its words), `unknown instance`, `no window`, `several windows`, `NO_PICTURE` (the window has no `FF_UIWindow`), `the window did not come on top` (`NOT_ON_TOP`, round 3: the take's posted z-order change never landed within `FIND_MS`), `the window worker stopped`; a list's `the devices nest too deep` (`TOO_DEEP`); and `left open in Live`, the holder's closed reason only when its close may have left the editor open in Live (`close::Shut::LeftOpen`: the guard could not tap, the ref's turn-off failed or got no answer, the path check was not certain, the set at the path failed; the `closed` record keeps the close's own why). A close that found nothing of the hub's open in Live (`Shut::NoneOpen`: the device deleted, or no open Pro-Q 4 after a move) keeps its own reason (round 3). Every one is a constant in `fohmixer_proto::eq::reason` (the hub's `plugwin::NO_WINDOW` / `SEVERAL` / `STOPPED` / `NOT_ON_TOP`, `win::NO_PICTURE` and `router::eq::UNKNOWN_INSTANCE` / `TOO_DEEP` are those constants) with its Slovak sentence in the UI's one table (`behave::eq::reason_text`), read by a card's note (`failure_text`; `locked` and `in use` are quiet there: the card's lock says it) and by the cards' list note (`list_text`); anything else is a failure's own text.
  - **The frames are binary WebSocket messages:** a 28-byte header, the session, the frame's number in that session from 1, the page's last input the picture shows (PR J), and the part's corner and the whole picture's size (PR L) (`eq::FrameHead`, each a big-endian `u32`), then the JPEG (`eq::frame`, `eq::frame_parts`). The outbox keeps only the newest picture (`Outbox::eq_frame`, one slot, with its session; the header goes on as the frame is taken, numbered by the flight), and the writer takes it together with the batch's messages (`Outbox::next_batch` → `Batch`) and writes it after them, **one in flight per page** (PR I, below): the next goes only once the page acked the frames before it (`eq_frame_ack {session, seq}`, straight to the outbox: `Outbox::frame_acked`; an ack acks every frame up to `seq`) or, with one in flight, nothing was heard of it for `outbox::flow::FRAME_ACK_WAIT` (1 s); never a third. Before PR I the slot acted only before the writer: a link slower than the picture's changes queued frames in the kernel's send buffer, seconds of them, with every pong and ack behind them. A closed `eq` forgets a waiting frame and the flight, and hands its round trips to the close's `frames` record. The page acks a frame of its open editor with its number as it arrives (before decoding, `store::eq::frame_ack`) and drops one whose session is not its open editor's (no ack).
  - **A card's last picture:** `GET /api/eq/picture?instance=&path=` with the token (`image/jpeg`, `no-store`; 404 before a first frame). The CSP allows `img-src blob:`.
- **Discovery** (`eq/walk.rs`): the strip's track is walked through the script's `get_prop`, one batch per level. A device of the script's class `PluginDevice` whose `class_display_name` is `Pro-Q 4` is listed (`PRODUCT`; it holds when renamed) with its `$ref` (`Found::id`, from the list answer's item, `walk::Item`). A `RackDevice`'s chains are walked down to 3 racks deep (`MAX_RACKS`), 8 batches a walk at most (`MAX_READS`). The path is Live's index form (`live_set tracks 1 devices 1 chains 0 devices 0`), the place `Vocal FX › Main`, or `na tracku`. A path not in a list the hub gave is refused (`unknown`).
  - The state keeps each list on its own, by instance and the track's LOM target, with each device (`eq::Device`: its name and `$ref`; `Eqs::listed`, `name_of`). A track's new list replaces its last one and takes the paths it names from every other list: a path belongs to the newest list that names it (a track or a device moved, so a strip not listed since may still hold the path of the device another strip has now; with the first list in map order an open of that other strip compared its device with the stale name and was refused `moved`, round-2 review).
  - **A card's picture is its device's** (round 3): after every list and every connect the router hands the cards' pictures the device each listed path names by its `$ref` (`Eqs::named` → `Pictures::named`): a picture whose path names another device now, or none, goes. By `$ref`, not by name: two Pro-Q 4s that keep their default name change places when a track moves above them. The frame sink puts a picture only while its path still names the device the open read (`Pictures::put` with the open's ref, `open::ref_id`), so a captured editor whose device moved away no longer brings it back with its next frame.
  - **A connect forgets** (`Router::eq_connected` on `LiveEvent::Connected`): the instance's lists (so its cards' pictures, through `Pictures::named`) and its editors' refs. On `Connected`, not `Disconnected`: another set and the script's new registry show only from the new connection on, and a walk answered from the old one after its `Disconnected` would otherwise list old paths again.
- **The state** (`eq.rs`, pure, tested):
  - **One editor on the PC's screen at a time** (round-3 ruling; the owner allowed it on 2026-10-09): the PC has one screen and one cursor, and two taken editors are both on top and overlap at 1349 × 809 (a picture showing the other's pixels, refused touches, a covered inert spot). While a client holds any editor (waiting for its switch, opening, open or closing), another client's open is refused: that editor `locked`, any other `in use` (`Eqs::in_use`). The same client's open of another editor switches (its own closes first, `switch`). So one open runs at a time (started at once, or once its client's switch closed: `Eqs::start`; no queue) and one finger is on the screen. The pages' cards all read ZAMKNUTÉ meanwhile (`behave::eq::card_lock`: `InUse`). Faders, mute and pan stay free for everyone.
  - **Contacts are numbered** (`Eqs::contacts`, one counter): the number rides `Act::Touch`, `Command::Touch` and `PlugwinEvent::ContactEnded`, and `contact_ended` ends only that session's contact of that number (a late word of an older contact never ends a newer one).
  - A resting contact is the window worker's to keep alive (below); the router re-sends nothing.
  - A holder silent for 2 s (`SILENT_MS`: no message at all, pings included) gets its contact cancelled (the router's tick, `deck::TICK`).
  - A detached page's editor closes (`detach`).
  - Records go to the event log as `eq` (warn class): `take`, `open`, `resize` (PR G: `w`, `h` asked, `client_w`, `client_h`, `ok`, `ms`, `reverted`: an unlanded one's rectangle before posted back, #74 review; an open's before its `opened`), `opened`, `sized` (PR G: `w`, `h`: a grab of another size, no resize on its way), `touch` (down, up, cancel; never a move), `silent`, `close` (why), `closed`, `lost`, `failed` (why: `moved`, `open on the PC`, …), `refused` (why: `unknown`, `locked`, `in use`), `problem` (the close may have left the editor open in Live), `none_open` (the close found nothing open: its device deleted; a path check that finds no open Pro-Q 4 after a move is a `problem` instead, since the editor may sit deeper than the walk looks), `moved` (with `to`, only from the fallback path check), `contact_ended` (with `contact`), and once a minute per editor `rate` (grabs, sent, failed, grab and encode ms, bytes, size, `gap_ms`: the largest gap between two injections of a contact; PR I: its holder's `frame_trips`, `frame_rtt_ms`, `frame_rtt_max_ms`, the round trips of the frames sent alone to their acks, and `ack_waits`, the frames that went because the ack's wait ran out, taken from the holder's outbox, `Outbox::take_trips`, `Eqs::holder_of`; the PR I review: the worker reports an editor's last part of a minute as a `rate` when it lets the editor go, released or its window gone, `Worker::last_rate`, and a `rate` whose holder is gone carries no trip fields), and at a close `frames` (the holder's trips since the last `rate`, handed back by `Outbox::forget_eq`; none when nothing was measured or waited out, `router::eq::frames_fields`).
- **The open's read** (`eq/open.rs`, pure, tested; `open_editor`): before `is_editor_open = true`, one batch reads the device at the listed path: `class_display_name`, `name`, `is_editor_open`, and its parent's `devices` (the item at its index, when its `path` is the listed one, gives the device's `$ref`; the script registers every object it encodes).
  - Not a Pro-Q 4, or not the name the list found there: refused `moved`.
  - Its editor already open in Live (opened on the PC, left open by a failed guard or the hub's stop): refused `open on the PC`. The hub has no window of it, so it never takes it and never turns it off without the guard; adopting an already-open window is not done (the page says to close it on the PC).
  - Else the editor is opened through the `$ref` (`{"$ref", "class": "PluginDevice"}`), and a take that fails turns it off again through the ref (this open turned it on: the read found it closed).
- **The close sequence** (`router/eq.rs` `close_editor`), in this order:
  1. The guard: its editor looked up first (a guard of a window already lost fails at once and ends no contact: round-2 review), then its own contact ends at its last point (an up), another session's contact is cancelled at its last point and reported (`ContactEnded`, why `GUARD_CANCEL`: the PC injects one contact, so the tap would fail or end the other engineer's drag), the frames stop, the editor is placed at 1349 × 809 in two steps, each seen to land (PR G and the #74 review, above; a step not seen within 1 s: the guard fails, `NOT_BACK`), and the inert spot (546, 15) is tapped (down, 30 ms, up). This closes a text field Pro-Q 4.02 may have open.
  2. A wait of 300 ms (`GUARD_WAIT`).
  3. `is_editor_open = false`, **only if the guard succeeded.** A failed guard leaves the editor open in Live (`problem`), never closed with a field possibly open. What the close left in Live is a `close::Shut`: `Off`, `NoneOpen` (nothing of the hub's open there: its holder hears its own close, record `none_open`) or `LeftOpen` (its holder hears `left open in Live`, record `problem`; round-3 review).
     - **Through the `$ref` the open read** (`EqIo::refs`, per session; `RouterMsg::EqOpened` carries it; kept only for the editor of that session, and only when its instance did not connect again since the open began: `Act::Open` and `EqOpened` carry the instance's connection number, `Eqs::connection`, which each connect counts up, since the script registers objects by Live pointer and a pointer registered anew can name another object; round-2 review; dropped when the close ends, the window is lost, or the instance connects again): the same object wherever it moved, so a close never turns off another engineer's editor that moved onto the held path.
     - **A ref turn-off that got no answer or failed otherwise** (Live offline, a timeout: its thread busy, and the set may still land; a refused request; any answer but the two below): Live is left alone (`LeftOpen`, `close::ref_failed`), never the path check, which takes any open Pro-Q 4 at the held path (another engineer's after a move; round-2 review). `close::ref_off` reads the answer (pure, tested).
     - **A ref whose device was deleted** (the script answers `errorType` `StaleRef`, `deleted` / `class changed`: `close::STALE_REF`; the pointer holding another class means the device went too): Live closed the editor with its device, so Live is left alone with nothing open (`NoneOpen`, `close::deleted`), never the path check: the held path can then only name another device (the one now at that path, its editor maybe open: turned off without its guard, the close-crash path; round-3 review).
     - **The fallback, only when the script does not know the ref or none is held** (`errorType` `UnknownRef`, `unknown id`: each connection starts a new registry, `close::UNKNOWN_REF`; no ref after a reconnect): the path check (`eq/close.rs`, pure, tested; `check_close`). The held path is Live's index form, which a track or device moved above it points elsewhere. One batch re-reads the device at the held path (`class_display_name`, `is_editor_open`). If it is still a Pro-Q 4 with its editor open, it closes there. Otherwise the set's tracks, return tracks and master track are walked for their Pro-Q 4s (the list's walk, bounded the same way, one track after another: only the fallback runs it) and their `is_editor_open` read in one batch. Exactly one open: the moved editor, closed at its new path, logged as a warning and recorded as `eq` `moved` with `to`. None open: nothing to close (`Check::NoneOpen` → `NoneOpen`). ## PR I: the owner's first iPad session (2026-10-11)

"Úplne nepoužiteľné": over the iPad's Wi-Fi the picture froze about 2.2 s every ~3 s and lagged seconds behind a finger, though a drag at full zoom moved the band on the PC at once. The pings' `n − rtt_n` ramped 2…22 about 30 times: no pong reached the page for 2.2 s at a time, while the hub received every ping on time (#71 comment 6105994608). The frames (about 9 a second at ~64 KB) piled up in the socket's send buffer, and every pong, ack and value waited behind them. The fix, in the same batch as the overview's ruling:

- **One frame in flight per page** (`outbox/flow.rs`, pure): the writer sends a page's next frame only once the frames before it were acked or, with one in flight, nothing was heard of it for `FRAME_ACK_WAIT` (1 s, from the last frame sent or ack read, whichever came later); never a third (`frame_due`). The frames are numbered (`Flight::sent` gives the number, the page's ack names the newest it got and acks every one up to it, `Flight::acked`), so the count of frames in flight is exact and a later ack covers a lost one. A page that never acks gets two frames, then none until it acks; a new session or a closed editor starts afresh. The writer sleeps while a frame waits (`Wake::At`, or `Woken` with two in flight, `next_batch`), and wakes for the ack, a message, or the wait's end.
- **The PR I review (fresh context, 1 high, 3 medium, 4 low, all fixed before the merge):** the wait let one frame a second into a stalled socket, so after a 3 s stall the pongs waited behind 3–4 frames again (now never a third, `flow.rs` `a_stalled_link_never_has_more_than_two_frames_in_flight`); the writer took the frame after writing its batch, so a reply put meanwhile (the session's `eq` open) went after its first frame (now taken with the batch, `outbox.rs` `a_message_put_before_a_frame_is_written_before_it`; "no frame while messages wait" was rejected: on a slow link values arrive during every batch's write and the frames would starve); a session's last minute of `rate` and frame trips was lost at a close (the worker's `last_rate`, the close's `frames` record); the loopback E2E could not fail without the flow control, so `tests/eq.rs` runs a page through a proxy whose downlink carries two frames a second (`support::throttle::Throttle`, the sim's four a second would pile up without the flow control). The UI's findings are in `.claude/rules/ui-rust.md` (the settle after a tap's zoom, the factor under 2×, CELÝ EQ during a drag, the fingers' event times).
- **Smaller frames:** q60 (`QUALITY`): a real Pro-Q 4 screenshot re-encoded at 60 is about 15 % smaller than at 70 (42.7 → 36.4 KB, 44.6 → 38.1 KB), its scale labels, curve and grid lines as readable magnified twice; at 55 the faint grid lines fade. The sim's flat picture is ~21 KB either way (a unit test bounds it at 24 KB). The picture keeps its size: a finger maps to its pixels. A lossless step left for later: `jpeg-encoder`'s optimized Huffman tables (a second pass; its encode time on the PC unmeasured).
- **The records to read the next session back:** the `rate` record's frame round trips and `ack_waits` (above), and the page's `eq_touch` / `eq_mv` events (each finger's down, up or cancel with its picture point and the zoom, its moves summarized; `.claude/rules/ui-rust.md`).
- **The PC check (on the real iPad over Wi-Fi):** from the event log while the EQ is open, the pings' `n − rtt_n` stays at most 2, the `rate` record's `frame_rtt_ms` / `frame_rtt_max_ms` and `ack_waits` say what the link did, and the `eq_touch` points lie on the band points the engineer meant (`trace` records of the page's socket).
