# Claps from the surface (#220; the design comment on #220 of 2026-11-16, mockup `docs/mockups/claps-v2.html`)

- **Why a device:** the FohMixer script runs in Live's ~30 Hz tick, so notes the hub sent would jitter by up to ~34 ms (audible at 200 BPM). The `FohMixer Claps` Max for Live MIDI effect (`live-device/FohMixer-Claps/`, first on the Claps track, before its Instrument Rack; `midiin` → `midiout` untouched) plays the pattern on Live's own grid: `metro 4n @quantize 4n` on Live's transport (silent while Live stops), `transport`'s beat and units made the bar's beat 0–3 (`expr ($i1 - 1 + ($f2 >= 240.)) % 4`), beat 0 turns an armed device into a running one, a running, not silent beat of the pattern plays each pitch `makenote 100 150` (ms, never a tempo length: a stop of Live's transport would freeze its note-off) → `noteout`. Its hello (`metro 1000` → `pack` → `deferlow` → `udpsend`, #240) leaves Live's audio thread before the socket write; the beat path stays timed. Its watchdog (#220 review): every word restarts a `delay <FORGET_MS>`; its end stops the device and sends `set 0` to the switch's `change`, so the hub's next `on 1` arms it again (a hub that died or stopped mid-song never leaves it clapping). It holds no decision beyond that: `scripts/check_claps_device.py` (CI's `python` job) holds every box and line (`BOXES`, `WIRING`, guarded inlets `ONLY`), the ports from the proto crate and the version. Built by `build_amxd.py` as a MIDI effect (header kind `mmmm`, `--device-port`); the install writes it into `Presets\MIDI Effects\Max MIDI Effect\` (`Get-FohDevicePresets`, `$script:MidiDevices`; `Test-FohAmxd` wants `mmmm` for it). Nothing in CI runs the patch: its behaviour is proven only on the PC.
- **The wire** (`fohmixer_proto::claps`): to the device UDP `127.0.0.1:39114` (`[claps] device_port`) `notes`, `pattern`, `silent`, `on` (OSC `,i…`, in that order: the pattern before the switch), at every change and every `SEND_EVERY_MS` (the device acts on a change only: `[change]` before `on`); the device's hello to `127.0.0.1:39113` (`[claps] port`, `claps_udp.rs`) `,isi`: wire 1, `v<version>`, running. `[claps] notes` (1–8 pitches, default `DEFAULT_NOTE` 50) is written by hand on the PC; the installer keeps the table (`Resolve-FohClaps`, before `[tls]`, after `[scenes]`). Test hubs and the E2E MBC hub run with both ports 0.
- **The rule** (`rules/claps.rs`, pure): `Mode` Off / Tapping {taps, last} / Armed {bar} / On. A tap (`tap(pos)`): 4/4, playing and a playhead known, else `Refusal`; snapped `beat_of` (round, mod 4). A bar after the last tap (or a jump back past it) ends the tapping: the taps are the pattern, Armed at the bar heard; a new bar → On. A stop ends a tapping and re-arms (`bar: None`: the next bar after the play's start); leaving 4/4 does too (the device is switched off, so it starts again at a bar). A dot while Off arms that beat; the last beat cleared stops. `words(silent, notes)`: `on` only Armed/On and 4/4. `phase(silent)`: `Signature` whenever not 4/4, else Tapping, NextBar/Waits, On/Clip/Waits. The device is off while tapping (a new tapping replaces the pattern).
- **The glue** (`router/claps.rs`): `CLAPS_CLIENT` (`ClientId::MAX - 8`, in the `Status` `own` lists) follows the Claps track's set's `current_song_time`, `tempo`, `signature_numerator`/`_denominator` and the track's `arrangement_clips` (`Layout::claps_track`: the first placed `+CLAPS` strip; a second `+CLAPS` in one set is `DuplicateClaps` on both and neither claps). The clips (start, end, `muted`) are read by `$ref` in chunks at every list value and every transport change (a clip switched on or off shows at the next start or stop). A tap's time is `tap_ms` (page `t` + the socket's offset, never after its arrival), its position `position_at` (the playhead heard moved on at the tempo, nothing past `STALE_MS`). `silent` = an active clip covers the playhead `SILENCE_AHEAD_BEATS` (0.5) ahead while Live plays (one tick was too late: the playhead is a tick old, the word takes the router and UDP, so the device clapped a clip's first beat too; whole beats only, so half a beat early silences no beat outside the clip). The track is known by its set and label (`track_change`): the same track at another index keeps what claps and follows its clips afresh (`Moved`); anything else stops it, the old device told `on 0` first (`claps_send` sends nothing once the track is gone). Pages get `ServerMsg::Claps` (phase, the shown pattern, the playhead while Live plays, the tempo) on a change or when the playhead is off their ring by over `RING_SLACK` 0.25 beat. Records `claps` (warn class): `tap`, `refused`, `beat`, `stop`, `pattern`, `on`, `silent`, `track`, `clips`, `device` (`gone` after `FORGET_MS`), `listen`. `/api/status` `claps`. MBC logins neither send nor get any of it (`ws/role.rs`).
- **The page** (`components/claps.rs`, `behave/claps.rs`, `claps.css`): on a `claps` strip once the hub told one; SEL's left and width, above the ARM key (`bottom: 64px`, narrow 72 px); the strip's `strip-side` ends 210 px above the zone's foot there. TAP is `HoldKey` with its `tap` (the down's own `dom::event_epoch`, sent at the lift); a dot acts on its `pointerdown`. The ring and the flash are drawn by a frame function from `LiveStore.claps` (state + the epoch it came at), never a timer. `claps.spec.ts` drives it on SimLive band track 0 tagged `"CLAPS" +G:CLAPS +CLAPS` in a CLAPS group on the FOH page.
- **SimLive:** `Clip.muted` (fixture `muted`, host line `clip mute <track> <clip> <0|1>`), `Song.signature_numerator`/`_denominator` (fixture `signature`, host line `song signature <n> <d>`); `tests/it/claps.rs` (`hub-hosts`) uses a UDP socket of its own as the device.
- **The beat count assumes the whole song is 4/4:** the device's bar comes from Live's transport, the hub's from `round(pos) mod 4`; a signature change earlier in the arrangement makes them disagree.
- **The PC steps (the main session's, after the merge):** place `FohMixer Claps` from the band user's `Presets\MIDI Effects\Max MIDI Effect` on the Claps track through the LOM, first in its chain (read the track's `latency_in_samples` before and after); tag its Tuner `+CLAPS` beside its strip marker; write `[claps] notes = [<the pitch most active clips use>]` by hand before `[tls]`; save the set; then `/api/status` `claps` (`track`, `signature` `4/4`, `device` with the version, `clips`). The patch's runtime is proven only there (CI checks its structure): play, tap, see the device's notes land on the grid (`metro @quantize`, `@autostart` from the middle of a song, `transport`'s outlet order units before beats), stop and play from the middle, `on 0` (a hold) stops it, a hub stop stops it within 3 s, and a clip's first beat claps once.
- **The first PC run (1.53.0, 2026-11-17, #220's comments):** the band set's `Claps #` is track 25; `[claps] notes = [53]` (all 38 active clips play 53, 11 also 52: legacy `Clip.get_notes(0, 0, 100000, 128)` through `cmd` gives `[pitch, time, length, velocity, muted]` lists, while `get_notes_extended` answers unencodable `MidiNote` objects). A MIDI effect's `load_item` lands first, before the Instrument Rack, with no `move_device`. Live's tracks have no `latency_in_samples` in the LOM: read each device's. The runtime check: start Live, THEN seek (`current_song_time` set while stopped is put back by AbleSet, and one past `song_length` is ignored), inside a 4/4 song with no active clip on the track; set the four dots; sample the track's `output_meter_level` every ~20 ms and count the whole beats (floor of the playhead) above 0.05: 72 of 72 in 34 s. Stop and put the playhead back; the rules' writes while playing leave the band set's `*`: save it.
