# Listen (#210: a track's sound, before its fader, in the browser)

## The design (phase 1, the design comment on #210 of 2026-11-15)

- **The device, `FohMixer Listen`** (`live-device/FohMixer-Listen/FohMixer Listen.maxpat`, built by `build_amxd.py --patch …`, `--port` rewrites its `udpsend` and `jit.net.send` boxes): silent (`sig~ 0.` into `plugout~`), in a rack's parallel chain like the LUFS devices. **It holds no clock (#264, the lane's design in comment 7184226996 on #264):** a Max for Live clock runs in Live's audio thread and its output (a `deferlow`, a `qmetro`'s hand-over to the low-priority queue) posts a window message from there, a click on the PA; the hub drives it, as the meter and the LUFS device, through `udpreceive @defer 1` (Max's main thread). It is pulled with their boxes (`scripts/listen_pull_check.py`'s `Shape` through `lufs_pull_check.pull_errors`): at `live.thisdevice`'s load (no `deferlow`) the load's `t` draws the poll port (`random 10000` `+ 40000`, the LUFS devices' range) into the `udpreceive` and the announce's last slot, sets the `is_active` observer (Device On: the gate's left inlet, as the meter's; a device whose rack is off answers nothing) and bangs the gate's right inlet once (it passes once the observer opened the gate: the announce at load), and draws the listen id's `random 21000` from its rightmost outlet, before its `path this_device` reads the Max id (`RANDOM_FIRST`, the review of the step: a fan out of `live.thisdevice` left that order to the boxes' places); Device On turning 1 opens the gate and bangs it, so a rack the hub switches on is learned at once. The hub sends a port two devices name (they drew one) one poll, on when either is the target's (`one_poll_a_port`), and counts such ports in `/api/status` `listen.shared_ports`. One `route /fohmixer/listen/poll /fohmixer/listen/frame /fohmixer/listen/move` (#264: the move's bang into `obj-poll-move` `t b b`, the load's port draw then the gate, so the device is off until the next poll; the hub's one `poll_ports::PortTable` finds a port two devices drew, `hub-rust.md`): the poll's `on` int through the gate into the pull `t b b b i`: the int first into `sel 1` (1 on: `change` → `sel 1 0` → `t b b`, the hello into `jit.net.send`'s right inlet, then `1` into the frame gate's left inlet; 0, or a bang's 0, off at once), then the re-read's `counter 0 39` (`poll_reread_check`, `Reread(40, 2)`: the four parents' paths every 40th poll, 10 s, at a count drawn at load), `dspstate~` (the sample rate), then the announce (`/fohmixer/listen ,iisssssiii`, wire 2, `udpsend 127.0.0.1 39111`) and the hello gate (the hello again while on). The frame poll's bang into the frame gate (`gate`, switched only by the `1`/`0` messages) bangs `jit.catch~ 2 @mode 1 @framesize 960` (whole 20 ms frames, every sample once, every whole frame caught since the bang before: a late or bunched poll puts more frames in one matrix, never loses one) → `jit.net.send @ip 127.0.0.1 @port 39111` (TCP), in Max's main thread as the `qmetro 10`'s output was. That `@mode 1` hands out every sample once in whole frames is Cycling '74's documentation, proved only by probe P3's `shapes` and `samples_per_s`. **The listen id** (`expr $i2 * 100000 + $i1 % 100000 + 1`: the device's Max id mixed with a `random 21000` drawn once at load) names the device in the announce and the hello: the Max id alone is numbered per Live process, and both Lives on the FOH PC announce to the same port (two equal ids would overwrite each other and one select would start both). **Each device has a port of its own** (#210's PC finding: every device bound `udpreceive 39112`, the devices of a port share one socket and a datagram reached only one of them). Two devices drawing one port lose one of them (Max's `udpreceive` cannot tell the patch a bind failed), a risk the LUFS and meter devices share. The identity is the LUFS devices' (`check_lufs_device.identity_errors` with `pulled` and the re-read: each name from `live.object` `get name` → `route name` → `zl reg` → `tosymbol`, read when the path gives an id, both `live.path` outlets into `route id`, and when a `live.observer` on the parent's `name` reports, its output only a `t b`; the set, parent 4, has no observer). `scripts/check_listen_device.py` (CI's `python` job) holds every one of these: the pull's boxes and lines, no `deferlow`, `defer`, `metro`, `qmetro`, `delay`, `del` or `pipe` anywhere (`poll_reread_check.still_errors`), no `maxurl`, the catch fed by `plugin~` and the frame gate alone, the select logic box by box and line by line (`BOXES`, `WIRING`, `SESSION_ONLY`: the listen id alone into the announce and the hello), the threads (`check_lufs_device.thread_errors`), the ports, frame, poll addresses and re-read count read from the proto crate. *History:* until #264 (1.60.4) the device asked `GET /api/listen/select?device=<id>` itself (`qmetro 250` → `dict.pack` → `maxurl`, `parse_type json`, `dict.unpack body:` / `on:`), held an `on` for 1 s (`delay 1000` → `deferlow`), announced each second (`metro 1000` → `deferlow`), read its parents again on its own `qmetro 10000` and streamed on a `qmetro 10`.
- **Editing a device's patch by script (any of the four, #240):** load the `.maxpat` with `json.loads` and write it back with `json.dumps(doc, indent="\t", ensure_ascii=False) + "\n"`: that round-trips every patch byte for byte, so the diff shows only the boxes and lines changed. Run the three device checks after each edit; they name a missing line or a timed box on the way to a send.
- **In the bundle since phase 2:** the `bundle` job builds `FohMixer Listen.amxd` with `build_amxd.py` beside the LUFS and meter devices, `New-FohBundle` (`$script:BundleFiles`, `$script:Devices`) carries it and `Install-FohUserDevices` writes it into each Live user's `Presets\Audio Effects\Max Audio Effect\` (held, never replaced, while a Live runs: `deploy-pc.md`); `Test-Fohmixer.ps1`'s device lists name three. Placing it on tracks is the main session's PC step, after the probe kit below.
- **The wire** is `fohmixer_proto::listen`: `wire` (version 2 since #264: the announce with its poll port last, checked by `lufs::wire::poll_port`, `poll_datagram(on)` and `frame_datagram()` with their golden bytes pinned by SimLive too, `POLL_MS` 250, `FRAME_POLL_MS` 20, `REREAD_POLLS` 40, `FORGET_MS` 3000, hello; a version 1 announce is refused, a version 1 hello is no hello; the LUFS wire's OSC readers shared), `jitnet` (the Jitter chunks; every number read in the order its own size field tells, the samples in the matrix header's), `frames` (`Framer`: 960-sample stereo frames, mono doubled, a non-finite value silence and counted (each matrix holding one is `/api/status` `listen.refused.non_finite`), a 96 kHz device decimated first), `resample` (the 2:1 decimator, below).
- **The hub** (`crates/fohmixer-hub/src/listen.rs` and `listen/`): `[listen] port` (39111, 0 off) binds TCP then UDP on the same number, 127.0.0.1 only (#256: `bind_with`; a port 0 takes up to `PAIR_TRIES` 16 TCP picks, the first whose UDP number binds: a free TCP number is not a free UDP one, Windows hands out both from one sequence and its excluded ranges differ per protocol; a fixed port is one pick. A test that needs a pick whose UDP number is taken binds TCP 0 and then holds UDP on that number, never the reverse: Windows refused a TCP bind on a number a UDP socket held, 10013, CI of a59888e5); an old `select_port` key is read and ignored (`config::ListenToml`), so a config of before still loads. **The polls** (`listen/poll.rs`, #264): one task and a socket of its own (`lufs_udp::poll_socket`, never read; `refused_sends`, `round_counts` shared): every `POLL_MS` `poll_round` sends each fresh device (`Listen::polls`: its port and whether it is the target's one device, `accepts`' choice) its `poll_datagram`, every `FRAME_POLL_MS` `frame_round` sends the selected device (`Listen::frame_port`) a `frame_datagram`, both clocks `MissedTickBehavior::Skip` (`clock`, pinned); at the start `sweep` sends `poll_datagram(false)` once to every port of `[listen]`'s `sweep` (`ListenCfg.sweep`, no config key, the LUFS devices' range by default, `serde(skip)` through `ListenToml`; a test hub sets `None` or its own device's port: a sweep reaches every socket of its range). `/api/status` `listen` `polls`, `frame_polls`, `poll_failed`, `swept`, `sweep_failed`, `poll_from`, and each device's `port`. The HTTP select route (`routes/listen.rs`, `RouteAccess::Device`, `select_refusals`, `Listen::select`, `selects`) is gone with #264: `/api/listen/select` has no role decision and is refused. `stream.rs`: at most 64 connections (`jit.net.send` may connect at load and stay: P2), each through `jitnet::Reader` (a matrix at most `MAX_DATA` = 50 × 960 × 2 × 4 = 384 000 bytes: 0.5 s at 96 kHz, what a `qmetro` held up half a second leaves in `jit.catch~`, never seconds of encoding on the async path; a matrix with a 0 dimension and no data is an empty matrix, consumed and answered, with data behind it `matrix_strides`); a chunk begun and not finished for 5 s (`stalled`, judged on every pass from the time the chunk BEGAN: its first bytes' read or the read that finished the chunk before it, so a byte every 0.9 s still ends at 5 s) ends its connection, and only a connection holding an unfinished chunk reads with the 1 s timer (an idle one just waits); a latency answer per matrix with `try_write` (never waits on Max; `answer_of`: a full socket drops it, counted, a part written ends the connection, `latency_broken`), the hello recorded only when it changes (`hello_news`), `take` the pure decision (`no_hello`, `sample_rate` other than 48000 or 96000, `not_selected`); an encoder per connection, made at its first frame (a failure kept on the connection: logged once, then `encoder` counted). `udp.rs`: announces in (at most 256 devices kept, `keeps`; more counted `devices`); the polls go out from `poll.rs`'s socket. `listen.rs`: the pure `choose` (one fresh device on the set's track; `two devices` / `no device`), `start_of` (one track at a time, **the newest start wins**, the main session's ruling on the review of the merged lane: a start of another track is `Take`; same-track starts share it). A take bumps the turn: one `listen` record `{what: taken, from, to, client, peer}` (`taken_fields`; `from`/`to` with instance, set, track and listeners), the pages of the old turn see it on `Listen::turns` (a `watch`) and are told `taken` with their track and stop, every packet carries the turn it was sent in (`session::passes`: a page hears only its own turn's), and a lease of an older turn leaves nothing when it drops. **A device is picked by its track's and Live set's names, never the instance** (accepted on the review): the target's set name is read again at every decision through `SetNames` (`set_name_of` over the Live handles), so a set loaded while a page listens is followed, a set unknown for a moment keeps the last name, and a track renamed meanwhile is no longer found. `session.rs`: `/ws/audio?token=` (any role, `Role::reaches` the instance; 503 `LISTEN_OFF` with port 0), `listen_start {instance, track}` (names 1 to 256 characters, `names_ok`) / `listen_stop`, `audio_status` back (`listening`, `stopped`, `no_source`, `taken`, `refused`), one Opus packet per 20 ms, `MAX_BEHIND` 4, `no_source` once after 2 s of silence and `listening` again before the next packet; every write within `ws::SEND_TIMEOUT` (`bounded`): a page that takes nothing (a locked iPad) is closed and its lease goes, so it never holds the one track. Records `listen` (warn class): `listening`, `start`/`stop` with the turn, `taken`, `hello`, `stream_up`/`stream_down`, `page`. `/api/status` `listen`.
- **The rack is on only while listened to (#238, the main session's design of 2026-11-17, approach 1):** dasio measured 29 idle devices (racks on, nobody listening, transport stopped) taking band Live's audio callback over its 2.67 ms period 796 times in 10 minutes (28 with the racks off), longest 18.4 ms, with missed and late blocks: each idle device keeps its `jit.catch~` on the audio thread and its `maxurl` asking 4 times a second. So the hub switches a track's `Listen` rack's Device On (`parameters 0` `value`, 1 or 0) with the lease, `listen/rack.rs`: on at a new target, BEFORE the page is told (the session awaits the switch's outcome; each LOM call bounded by the Live client's own 3 s), off when the last page of the track leaves (a stop, the socket gone), and at a take the new track's rack on first, then the old one's off (`start_switches`, `leave_switch`, pure; a join switches nothing; a take of the same track of the same Live, its set loaded anew, switches nothing off). The rack is the one `RackDevice` named exactly `Listen` among the `devices` of the track, the return track or the main track that carries the page's track name (one read of the three lists through the instance's handle, `rack_of`: a list answering `not found` is no track of that name there; `ambiguous` or any other refusal is its words), written at its index path (`<rack> parameters 0`; accepted on the lane's review: the read and the write are two calls milliseconds apart, so a device inserted or moved between them on that track could take the write; AUTOTUNE writes by `$ref` because its writes run unattended during a song). One task (`rack::run`) carries the switches out in the leases' order (queued under `Listen`'s state lock; one queue for both Lives, so a Live that does not answer delays the other Live's starts by up to two 3 s calls per switch ahead, and the starting page's socket waits with it), each one `listen` record `{what: rack, instance, track, on, result: done|missing|failed, problem}` and one line (warn unless done). **No rack** (none, two, or the track not found) answers the start `no_source` at once (`session::told_of`; the page keeps the track, `told_silent` set, so a device that comes is told `listening` before its first packet; a second page joining it switches nothing and is told `listening`, then `no_source` after the 2 s silence; its leave's off answers `missing` again); a write Live fails is the warn record only, the page told `listening`. The 2 s silence (`NO_SOURCE_MS`) counts from the switch-on's answer, never from before its wait (`Listening.last`), since the device only comes up then; until its first packet (about 0.3 s since #264: the device announces when Device On turns 1, the next round's poll says on, the next frame poll streams) the page shows the existing `no_source` hold, no new status (the design). **Nothing at the hub's start:** no walk of the tracks (#235: such a walk costs audio blocks), so a rack left on (a hub stopped while a page listened, a rack switched on by hand) stays on until a listen of its track ends. **The sets on the PC are saved with every `Listen` rack off** (the mitigation of 2026-11-17) and must stay so: a set saved with a rack on runs that device idle from the set's load, so save a set only while nobody listens (the hub's switch-on is in Live's set until the stop's off). A hub whose Listen port could not be bound switches nothing (`Listen::new`, no queue). SimLive honours it (`sim/listendev.py`: a device in a rack that is off answers no poll and streams nothing, and announces when it turns on; `listendev <where> <i> add rack off`, the `listenrack <where> <i>` line answers the rack's Device On); `tests/it/listen.rs`: `a_start_switches_the_tracks_listen_rack_on_and_the_stop_off`, `a_take_switches_the_new_tracks_rack_on_then_the_old_ones_off`, `with_two_pages_on_a_track_its_rack_goes_off_only_after_both_leave`, `a_track_without_a_listen_rack_or_an_unknown_instance_is_no_source`; the other tests' devices sit in racks that are on.
- **96 kHz (ROZHODNUTÉ on #210 of 2026-11-16, after the PC probe found the master Live at 96 kHz):** the hub takes 48000 and 96000 (`fohmixer_proto::listen::resample::decimation`: 1 and 2), every other rate is refused `sample_rate`. At 96 kHz a 2:1 `Decimator` (a 95-tap windowed-sinc FIR, Blackman, cut-off 21 kHz, unity at DC; flat within 0.05 dB to 15 kHz, 70 dB or more down from 24 kHz; 47 input samples of delay) runs per stream before the `Framer`, which is made for the hello's rate (`Framer::for_rate`, `stream::reframe`): two 10 ms device frames make one 20 ms Opus frame. `jitnet::MAX_DATA` is 50 × 960 × 2 × 4 = 384 000 bytes (0.5 s at 96 kHz; at 48 kHz 1 s, at most 50 Opus encodes on the async path for one matrix, about 10 to 20 ms). SimLive: `listendev <where> <i> add [rack] [<rate>]` (48000 by default; `MAX_FRAMES` 50); `tests/it/listen.rs` `a_device_at_96_khz_is_decimated_to_the_pages_48_khz_tone` and `a_device_at_another_rate_is_refused`. Never drop samples instead: everything above 24 kHz would fold into the audio band.
- **The encoder:** `opus` 0.4 (`opusic-sys` builds libopus 1.6 with CMake: the runners and the dev box have it; `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` carries libopus's BSD notice), 128 kbit/s, the restricted low-delay application.
- **Tests:** `tests/it/listen.rs` (SimLive's `sim/listendev.py`, version 2: `--listen-port`, each device binds a poll port of the range at its add (drawn again while taken: SimLive's only liberty), announces once while active, answers each poll on SimLive's main thread (`TICK_MS` 5) and streams the frames it owes at each frame poll only; host lines `listendev`, `tone`, `streamed`, `listenrack`, and `listenload` (every device Live runs announces once, as at a load: `hub()` sends it, the test hub starts after its host with its sweep off); the page decodes the hub's Opus back to the device's tone; `each_device_is_told_in_its_own_poll_and_only_the_selected_one_streams`) and `tests/it/listen_wire.rs` (host-free, also in the `windows` job with the lib's `listen::`; `a_device_heard_is_polled_with_its_select_and_forgotten_once_silent`, `a_hub_sweeps_its_range_once_with_off_at_its_start`: a fake device's socket in the range, datagrams read only from `listen.poll_from`). Both in the `hub-hosts` nextest group. A test hub binds Listen only when it asks (`TestHub::start` sets `listen.port = 0`). The tests take a port free for UDP and TCP (`free_port`, as the LUFS tests take theirs): the SimLive host needs the hub's Listen port when it starts. Decided against on the review (#210): an ephemeral hub port with SimLive told afterwards; the LUFS tests' pattern is kept until a race shows.

## The page (phase 2; the owner's approved mockup `docs/mockups/listen-v1.html`)

- **The decoder: WebCodecs `AudioDecoder` with `codec: "opus"`, 48 kHz stereo** (ROZHODNUTÉ on #210 of 2026-11-16): Safari 26 (iPadOS 26) added WebCodecs' AudioDecoder, the FOH iPad runs Safari 26.6.1, WebKit refuses Opus only above 2 channels (WebKit bug 327697), and Playwright's WebKit 26.0 and Chromium both decode it. It needs a secure context (the hub's https name, or `127.0.0.1` in the E2E): on the emergency plain-http address by IP the key says `no_decoder` and nothing opens. No wasm decoder.

The essential trace event `listen` (`diag::trace::listen`: `what` tap/follow/open/close/status, `phase`, the track).
- **Glue (`src/listen.rs`, mutants-excluded):** `Listener`, a context of `Surface` (outlives the detail: the foot names the track behind the mix), its own `/ws/audio?token=` socket (binary `arraybuffer`), the player through `listen_player.js` (a wasm-bindgen snippet: Trunk copies it to `dist/snippets/`), whose `startListenPlayer` runs INSIDE the key's tap (a browser starts a page's audio only in a user's gesture, and on a touch screen that is the LIFT: the key acts on `pointerup`, owns its touches); a strip bound by place has its name read meanwhile (`resolving` holds a second tap). `listen_player.js`: iemmixer's player adapted (an adaptive 80-500 ms jitter buffer, packets queued while the context is not running), the gain into `listen-limiter-worklet.js` (copied by `index.html`'s `copy-file`; -1 dBFS, the output is silent until it loaded), `window.__fohmixerListen()` (running, limiter, packets, decoded, played, dropouts, level, gain, error) for the E2E.
- **The look (`listen.css`, `components/listen.rs`):** `ListenKey` (`listen-key`, `data-state` the phase, `aria-pressed` lit) beside `ChannelSelect` in the detail's bar, grey and dimmed while idle, the accent while lit; `ListenVolume` (`listen-volume`, `data-value` 0-100, a pointer-captured slim track) only while lit; `ListenLine` (`listen-line`) in `ColumnFoot` after the scene's line. ## The PC checks (2026-11-17, v1.53.1; results on #210)

Announce each step to dasio first (the shared FOH PC, its CPU). Results on #210 without host names, track names, user names or paths. The probe tool (`tools/listen-probe`) is retired: it sent the UDP select the devices no longer hear, so the checks use the real hub.

- **Place** a device in a `Listen` rack at the end of the track (an empty `Dry` chain plus the device's chain) through the hub's `cmd`: `insert_device ["Audio Effect Rack", n]`, `insert_chain`, `load_item` of the browser item, `move_device` into chain 1 (the work product `listen-place.cjs` does it; an `insert_device` on a big set can answer `no result within 3 s` and still land, so read `devices` again before inserting a second rack). Read every device's `latency_in_samples` before and after.
- **Stream:** a client of `/ws/audio?token=` sends `{"type":"listen_start","instance":…,"track":<the track's name>}` and counts the binary Opus packets (50 a second, ~321 bytes each at 128 kbit/s); `/api/status` `listen` shows `devices` (`version`, `set`, `sample_rate`, `sending`), `selects`, `frames` and `refused`.
- **The sound itself:** decode the captured packets with Chromium's WebCodecs `AudioDecoder` (`opus`, 48 kHz stereo) on a routed https page and read peak and RMS: music reads like music (−12.8 dBFS peak, −25.9 dBFS RMS on the backing tracks); a wrong byte order would read as full-scale noise.
- **CPU:** dasio's sampler on the band Live's process, 7.5 s samples, A/B windows of the same song from the same spot, after Live has run a while (the first window after a restart reads ~140 % high from warm-up). One streaming device measured 432 % → 433 % (no measurable cost).
- **The racks off while idle (#238):** with every `Listen` rack off and nobody listening, dasio's callbacks over the 2.67 ms period as in #238's OFF window (28 in 10 minutes, no missed or late block, transport stopped); a listen streams within about 2 s of the tap (the device comes up, announces, polls) and its rack reads Device On 0 again within 1 s of the stop (the hub's `listen` `rack` records, `on` true then false, `result` done); read `latency_in_samples` of the track's devices with the rack off and on. **SimLive's off device is the design's model, not yet a PC fact:** with every rack off, read `/api/status` `listen.devices` and `selects` over a few seconds and record on #238 whether a device in a rack that is off still announces or polls (Max's scheduler may run on while the device's audio is off); SimLive then follows what the PC showed (`live-script.md`).
