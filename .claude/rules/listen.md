# Listen (#210: a track's sound, before its fader, in the browser)

## The design (phase 1, the design comment on #210 of 2026-11-15)

- **The device, `FohMixer Listen`** (`live-device/FohMixer-Listen/FohMixer Listen.maxpat`, built by `build_amxd.py --patch …`, `--port` rewrites its `udpsend` and `jit.net.send` boxes, `--hub-port` its select poll's URL): silent (`sig~ 0.` into `plugout~`), in a rack's parallel chain like the LUFS devices. `plugin~` → `jit.catch~ 2 @mode 1 @framesize 960` (whole 20 ms frames, every sample once) banged by a `qmetro 10` (the low-priority queue: the matrix and the socket write stay off the audio thread) that runs only while selected → `jit.net.send @ip 127.0.0.1 @port 39111` (TCP). That `@mode 1` hands out every sample once in whole frames is Cycling '74's documentation, proved only by probe P3's `shapes` and `samples_per_s`. **The listen id** (`expr $i2 * 100000 + $i1 % 100000 + 1`: the device's Max id mixed with a `random 21000` drawn once at load) names the device in the announce, the hello and its select poll: the Max id alone is numbered per Live process, and both Lives on the FOH PC announce to the same port (the review of the lane: two equal ids would overwrite each other and one select would start both). **The select is asked over HTTP, by each device for itself** (the DECIDED comment on #210 of 2026-11-17, after the PC's P1 failed: every device bound `udpreceive 39112`, the devices of a port share one socket, and a datagram reached only one of them, so the band Live's two devices never streamed; a select port picked per device was rejected: Max's `udpreceive` cannot tell the patch a bind failed; broadcast or multicast on loopback is unreliable on Windows): the listen id through `change` (once, when it is first known) and `t b l` sets the url (`sprintf http://127.0.0.1:8480/api/listen/select?device=%ld` into the hot inlet of a `dict.pack url: http_method: parse_type:`, `get` and `json` from two message boxes at `live.thisdevice`'s load: one request) and then starts a `qmetro 250` (#240: the low-priority queue; a `metro` ran each poll's `dict.pack` and request start on Live's audio thread) that bangs the `dict.pack` into `maxurl` (no request goes without a URL, and the id's refresh each second asks nothing more: 4 requests a second); its answer's `dict.unpack body:` then `dict.unpack on:` (Max's dict holds JSON `true` as 1) into `sel 1`: 1 restarts the `delay 1000` (four polls; its end passes a `deferlow`, #240, so the off and nothing after it runs on the audio thread) and turns it on, an `on` of any other value turns it off at once; no `on` in the answer (an error answer: 400, 403, 503; a body that is no JSON) or a failed request is no answer, and the hold ends it within 1 s (the decision's "a failed request means off"; one lost request on loopback never cuts the stream). SimLive does exactly that (`answers_on`: True, False or None). That `maxurl`'s `parse_type json` and `dict.unpack` of a sub-dictionary behave so is Cycling '74's documentation, proved only by the PC check. Not a `regexp` on the body text: its match comes out of its centre outlet (2), no match out of outlet 3, and Cycling '74 says unescaped double quotes are never matched, so a JSON body is read as a dictionary, never as text (and a page's HTML answering an old hub's route has no `on`). A change to on sends the hello (`fohmixer_listen 1 <listen id> v<version> <sample rate>`) into `jit.net.send`'s right inlet first, then starts the `qmetro`; while on, the hello goes again each second (the hub records only a change: `stream::hello_news`). The identity (`live.path` id and four parents, the meter's section copied: read at load and on a change only, #240, `check_lufs_device.identity_errors`) is kept in the announce's cold inlets, and `metro 1000` → `deferlow` → `t b b` bangs `dspstate~` (the sample rate) and then the announce each second, reading nothing from Live (the hub forgets a device after 3 s), into the announce (`/fohmixer/listen ,iisssssii`, `udpsend 127.0.0.1 39111`). `scripts/check_listen_device.py` (CI's `python` job) holds every one of these, the threads (`check_lufs_device.thread_errors`, #240: no timed box, `metro`, `delay`, `peakamp~`, `snapshot~` and the like, reaches a hot inlet on the way to a `maxurl`, `jit.net.send` or `udpsend` without a `deferlow` or `qmetro` between; a cold inlet only stores), the select logic box by box and line by line (`BOXES`, `WIRING`, `SESSION_ONLY`: the listen id alone into the announce, the hello and the poll's `sprintf`), no `udpreceive` at all, the ports, frame, hold, poll and path read from the proto crate and the hub's HTTP port from `config.rs` `DEFAULT_HTTP_PORT`.
- **In the bundle since phase 2:** the `bundle` job builds `FohMixer Listen.amxd` with `build_amxd.py` beside the LUFS and meter devices, `New-FohBundle` (`$script:BundleFiles`, `$script:Devices`) carries it and `Install-FohUserDevices` writes it into each Live user's `Presets\Audio Effects\Max Audio Effect\` (held, never replaced, while a Live runs: `deploy-pc.md`); `Test-Fohmixer.ps1`'s device lists name three. Placing it on tracks is the main session's PC step, after the probe kit below.
- **The wire** is `fohmixer_proto::listen`: `wire` (announce, the select's `SELECT_PATH`, `POLL_MS` 250, `SELECT_HOLD_MS` 1000 and `select_body`, hello; the LUFS wire's OSC readers shared), `jitnet` (the Jitter chunks; every number read in the order its own size field tells, the samples in the matrix header's), `frames` (`Framer`: 960-sample stereo frames, mono doubled, a non-finite value silence and counted (each matrix holding one is `/api/status` `listen.refused.non_finite`), a 96 kHz device decimated first), `resample` (the 2:1 decimator, below).
- **The hub** (`crates/fohmixer-hub/src/listen.rs` and `listen/`): `[listen] port` (39111, 0 off) binds TCP then UDP on the same number, 127.0.0.1 only; an old `select_port` key is read and ignored (`config::ListenToml`), so a config of before still loads. **The select route** (`routes/listen.rs`): `GET /api/listen/select?device=<listen id>`, `RouteAccess::Device` (no token), answered only to a loopback peer with no proxy header (`from_device`: the tunnel connects from 127.0.0.1, so the address alone never decides; else 403 `LOOPBACK_ONLY` and a warn line, `REFUSED_LINES` 8 per `ration::GAP`, `HubInner::select_refusals`), the path `wire::SELECT_PATH` in the route, the role table and the device check, `{"on":true|false}` (`select_body`) from `Listen::select` (`accepts`: the target's one device by `choose`, the same state the stream takes; counted `selects`), 400 `BAD_DEVICE`, 503 `LISTEN_OFF` with port 0. `stream.rs`: at most 64 connections (`jit.net.send` may connect at load and stay: P2), each through `jitnet::Reader` (a matrix at most `MAX_DATA` = 50 × 960 × 2 × 4 = 384 000 bytes: 0.5 s at 96 kHz, what a `qmetro` held up half a second leaves in `jit.catch~`, never seconds of encoding on the async path; a matrix with a 0 dimension and no data is an empty matrix, consumed and answered, with data behind it `matrix_strides`); a chunk begun and not finished for 5 s (`stalled`, judged on every pass from the time the chunk BEGAN: its first bytes' read or the read that finished the chunk before it, so a byte every 0.9 s still ends at 5 s) ends its connection, and only a connection holding an unfinished chunk reads with the 1 s timer (an idle one just waits); a latency answer per matrix with `try_write` (never waits on Max; `answer_of`: a full socket drops it, counted, a part written ends the connection, `latency_broken`), the hello recorded only when it changes (`hello_news`), `take` the pure decision (`no_hello`, `sample_rate` other than 48000 or 96000, `not_selected`); an encoder per connection, made at its first frame (a failure kept on the connection: logged once, then `encoder` counted). `udp.rs`: announces in (at most 256 devices kept, `keeps`; more counted `devices`); the hub sends the devices nothing. `listen.rs`: the pure `choose` (one fresh device on the set's track; `two devices` / `no device`), `start_of` (one track at a time, **the newest start wins**, the main session's ruling on the review of the merged lane: a start of another track is `Take`; same-track starts share it). A take bumps the turn: one `listen` record `{what: taken, from, to, client, peer}` (`taken_fields`; `from`/`to` with instance, set, track and listeners), the pages of the old turn see it on `Listen::turns` (a `watch`) and are told `taken` with their track and stop, every packet carries the turn it was sent in (`session::passes`: a page hears only its own turn's), and a lease of an older turn leaves nothing when it drops. **A device is picked by its track's and Live set's names, never the instance** (accepted on the review): the target's set name is read again at every decision through `SetNames` (`set_name_of` over the Live handles), so a set loaded while a page listens is followed, a set unknown for a moment keeps the last name, and a track renamed meanwhile is no longer found. `session.rs`: `/ws/audio?token=` (any role, `Role::reaches` the instance; 503 `LISTEN_OFF` with port 0), `listen_start {instance, track}` (names 1 to 256 characters, `names_ok`) / `listen_stop`, `audio_status` back (`listening`, `stopped`, `no_source`, `taken`, `refused`), one Opus packet per 20 ms, `MAX_BEHIND` 4, `no_source` once after 2 s of silence and `listening` again before the next packet; every write within `ws::SEND_TIMEOUT` (`bounded`): a page that takes nothing (a locked iPad) is closed and its lease goes, so it never holds the one track. Records `listen` (warn class): `listening`, `start`/`stop` with the turn, `taken`, `hello`, `stream_up`/`stream_down`, `page`. `/api/status` `listen`.
- **The rack is on only while listened to (#238, the main session's design of 2026-11-17, approach 1):** dasio measured 29 idle devices (racks on, nobody listening, transport stopped) taking band Live's audio callback over its 2.67 ms period 796 times in 10 minutes (28 with the racks off), longest 18.4 ms, with missed and late blocks: each idle device keeps its `jit.catch~` on the audio thread and its `maxurl` asking 4 times a second. So the hub switches a track's `Listen` rack's Device On (`parameters 0` `value`, 1 or 0) with the lease, `listen/rack.rs`: on at a new target, BEFORE the page is told (the session awaits the switch's outcome; each LOM call bounded by the Live client's own 3 s), off when the last page of the track leaves (a stop, the socket gone), and at a take the new track's rack on first, then the old one's off (`start_switches`, `leave_switch`, pure; a join switches nothing; a take of the same track of the same Live, its set loaded anew, switches nothing off). The rack is the one `RackDevice` named exactly `Listen` among the `devices` of the track, the return track or the main track that carries the page's track name (one read of the three lists through the instance's handle, `rack_of`: a list answering `not found` is no track of that name there; `ambiguous` or any other refusal is its words), written at its index path (`<rack> parameters 0`; accepted on the lane's review: the read and the write are two calls milliseconds apart, so a device inserted or moved between them on that track could take the write; AUTOTUNE writes by `$ref` because its writes run unattended during a song). One task (`rack::run`) carries the switches out in the leases' order (queued under `Listen`'s state lock; one queue for both Lives, so a Live that does not answer delays the other Live's starts by up to two 3 s calls per switch ahead, and the starting page's socket waits with it), each one `listen` record `{what: rack, instance, track, on, result: done|missing|failed, problem}` and one line (warn unless done). **No rack** (none, two, or the track not found) answers the start `no_source` at once (`session::told_of`; the page keeps the track, `told_silent` set, so a device that comes is told `listening` before its first packet; a second page joining it switches nothing and is told `listening`, then `no_source` after the 2 s silence; its leave's off answers `missing` again); a write Live fails is the warn record only, the page told `listening`. The 2 s silence (`NO_SOURCE_MS`) counts from the switch-on's answer, never from before its wait (`Listening.last`), since the device only comes up then; until its first packet (about 1 to 2 s: it announces each second and polls every 250 ms) the page shows the existing `no_source` hold, no new status (the design). **Nothing at the hub's start:** no walk of the tracks (#235: such a walk costs audio blocks), so a rack left on (a hub stopped while a page listened, a rack switched on by hand) stays on until a listen of its track ends. **The sets on the PC are saved with every `Listen` rack off** (the mitigation of 2026-11-17) and must stay so: a set saved with a rack on runs that device idle from the set's load, so save a set only while nobody listens (the hub's switch-on is in Live's set until the stop's off). A hub whose Listen port could not be bound switches nothing (`Listen::new`, no queue). SimLive honours it (`sim/listendev.py`: a device in a rack that is off neither announces nor streams; `listendev <where> <i> add rack off`, the `listenrack <where> <i>` line answers the rack's Device On); `tests/it/listen.rs`: `a_start_switches_the_tracks_listen_rack_on_and_the_stop_off`, `a_take_switches_the_new_tracks_rack_on_then_the_old_ones_off`, `with_two_pages_on_a_track_its_rack_goes_off_only_after_both_leave`, `a_track_without_a_listen_rack_or_an_unknown_instance_is_no_source`; the other tests' devices sit in racks that are on.
- **96 kHz (ROZHODNUTÉ on #210 of 2026-11-16, after the PC probe found the master Live at 96 kHz):** the hub takes 48000 and 96000 (`fohmixer_proto::listen::resample::decimation`: 1 and 2), every other rate is refused `sample_rate`. At 96 kHz a 2:1 `Decimator` (a 95-tap windowed-sinc FIR, Blackman, cut-off 21 kHz, unity at DC; flat within 0.05 dB to 15 kHz, 70 dB or more down from 24 kHz; 47 input samples of delay) runs per stream before the `Framer`, which is made for the hello's rate (`Framer::for_rate`, `stream::reframe`): two 10 ms device frames make one 20 ms Opus frame. `jitnet::MAX_DATA` is 50 × 960 × 2 × 4 = 384 000 bytes (0.5 s at 96 kHz; at 48 kHz 1 s, at most 50 Opus encodes on the async path for one matrix, about 10 to 20 ms). SimLive: `listendev <where> <i> add [rack] [<rate>]` (48000 by default; `MAX_FRAMES` 50); `tests/it/listen.rs` `a_device_at_96_khz_is_decimated_to_the_pages_48_khz_tone` and `a_device_at_another_rate_is_refused`. Never drop samples instead: everything above 24 kHz would fold into the audio band.
- **The encoder:** `opus` 0.4 (`opusic-sys` builds libopus 1.6 with CMake: the runners and the dev box have it; `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` carries libopus's BSD notice), 128 kbit/s, the restricted low-delay application.
- **Tests:** `tests/it/listen.rs` (SimLive's `sim/listendev.py`: `--listen-port`, `--hub-port` or the `listenhub <port>` line once the test hub has its port (`hub()` sends it), host lines `listendev`, `tone`, `streamed`; each device polls the route on a thread of its own every 250 ms, an answer whose JSON `on` is true selects it for 1 s; the page decodes the hub's Opus back to the device's tone; `each_device_asks_the_hub_whether_it_is_selected` the route for two devices, a proxy refused) and `tests/it/listen_wire.rs` (host-free, also in the `windows` job with the lib's `listen::`; the route's 400 and 503). Both in the `hub-hosts` nextest group. A test hub binds Listen only when it asks (`TestHub::start` sets `listen.port = 0`). The tests take a port free for UDP and TCP (`free_port`, as the LUFS tests take theirs): the SimLive host needs the hub's Listen port when it starts. Decided against on the review (#210): an ephemeral hub port with SimLive told afterwards; the LUFS tests' pattern is kept until a race shows.

## The page (phase 2; the owner's approved mockup `docs/mockups/listen-v1.html`)

- **The decoder: WebCodecs `AudioDecoder` with `codec: "opus"`, 48 kHz stereo** (ROZHODNUTÉ on #210 of 2026-11-16): Safari 26 (iPadOS 26) added WebCodecs' AudioDecoder, the FOH iPad runs Safari 26.6.1, WebKit refuses Opus only above 2 channels (WebKit bug 327697), and Playwright's WebKit 26.0 and Chromium both decode it. It needs a secure context (the hub's https name, or `127.0.0.1` in the E2E): on the emergency plain-http address by IP the key says `no_decoder` and nothing opens. No wasm decoder.
- **Pure (`behave/listen.rs`, tests `behave/listen/tests.rs`):** `Listen` (the wanted `Target {instance, track, label}`, the `Phase`, whether the socket is open; every tap's start and every follow is a numbered ask (`tap` gives `Act::Play` and the ask, `follow` an ask while lit, `answer(n, target)`: only the newest ask's answer acts, so a slow name read never starts or follows a channel the detail no longer shows, a tap while a start's name is read stops it, and a channel shown while a start waits is the one started); `opened`, `closed` (`Reopen(turn)` after `RECONNECT_MS` 1 s while wanted, `reopen_due(turn)`; the first `MAX_FAILED_OPENS` 3 opens after a tap that all close before they open end it `Unavailable` (a hub with `[listen] port = 0` answers 503), while once a socket opened a loss is an outage and it reopens for as long as it lasts), `status` (the hub's `listening`/`no_source`/`taken` only for the wanted track, `refused` for it or naming none: `Close`, `Silence`; a late word of a track asked before a follow does nothing); `lift_taps` (the key's lift of the finger that went down on it, inside the key) and `inside_box`; `track_name` (a strip bound by name gives the hub's name at once; one by place, a Tuner marker strip or the master, reads `get_prop name` of `live_set tracks N` / `return_tracks N` / `master_track` first: the hub picks a device by its track's NAME), `name_of`, `foot_text` (`listening <label>`, `<label>: no source`, `: reconnecting`, `: taken by another page`, `: refused`, `no audio decoder in this browser`), `gain_db`/`gain` (0 silence, else -40 to +12 dB on a straight line; `VOLUME_DEFAULT` 0.7), `volume_at`. `net::audio_url`. The essential trace event `listen` (`diag::trace::listen`: `what` tap/follow/open/close/status, `phase`, the track).
- **Glue (`src/listen.rs`, mutants-excluded):** `Listener`, a context of `Surface` (outlives the detail: the foot names the track behind the mix), its own `/ws/audio?token=` socket (binary `arraybuffer`), the player through `listen_player.js` (a wasm-bindgen snippet: Trunk copies it to `dist/snippets/`), whose `startListenPlayer` runs INSIDE the key's tap (a browser starts a page's audio only in a user's gesture, and on a touch screen that is the LIFT: the key acts on `pointerup`, owns its touches); a strip bound by place has its name read meanwhile (`resolving` holds a second tap). `listen_player.js`: iemmixer's player adapted (an adaptive 80-500 ms jitter buffer, packets queued while the context is not running), the gain into `listen-limiter-worklet.js` (copied by `index.html`'s `copy-file`; -1 dBFS, the output is silent until it loaded), `window.__fohmixerListen()` (running, limiter, packets, decoded, played, dropouts, level, gain, error) for the E2E.
- **The look (`listen.css`, `components/listen.rs`):** `ListenKey` (`listen-key`, `data-state` the phase, `aria-pressed` lit) beside `ChannelSelect` in the detail's bar, grey and dimmed while idle, the accent while lit; `ListenVolume` (`listen-volume`, `data-value` 0-100, a pointer-captured slim track) only while lit; `ListenLine` (`listen-line`) in `ColumnFoot` after the scene's line. Mounting the key on a detail follows that channel while listening (`Listener::follow`): another strip's detail opened by SEL, or the channel selector's pick.
- **E2E (`e2e/tests/listen.spec.ts`, both projects):** the harness's `--listen-port` (CI 39197: the FOH hub's `[listen]` and the band host's SimLive devices, which ask the FOH hub's `--http-port`; the MBC hub's config sets `[listen] port = 0`, else its default 39111 meets its own LUFS port) and the host lines `listendev`, `tone`. Both SimLive hosts load the same set name, so the hub finds a band device for master's track of the same name: a "no source" check uses a band track with no device (`B-Main repro #`). The page's player is read through `window.__fohmixerListen()` (a stop is proved by `running` false and `packets` standing still, not only by the hub's target); the tap's `listen` event names no track (it asks), its `answer` does. A local run needs the harness with the Listen ports and the MBC hub (the marker test waits for `MBC_MARKERS`), ports under 32768. A local browser probe: Chromium has no `AudioDecoder` on `about:blank` (no secure context), WebKit 26 has it there: probe on a secure origin (`page.route` to an https name, `e2e.md`).

## The PC checks (2026-11-17, v1.53.1; results on #210)

Announce each step to dasio first (the shared FOH PC, its CPU). Results on #210 without host names, track names, user names or paths. The probe tool (`tools/listen-probe`) is retired: it sent the UDP select the devices no longer hear, so the checks use the real hub.

- **Place** a device in a `Listen` rack at the end of the track (an empty `Dry` chain plus the device's chain) through the hub's `cmd`: `insert_device ["Audio Effect Rack", n]`, `insert_chain`, `load_item` of the browser item, `move_device` into chain 1 (the work product `listen-place.cjs` does it; an `insert_device` on a big set can answer `no result within 3 s` and still land, so read `devices` again before inserting a second rack). Read every device's `latency_in_samples` before and after.
- **Stream:** a client of `/ws/audio?token=` sends `{"type":"listen_start","instance":…,"track":<the track's name>}` and counts the binary Opus packets (50 a second, ~321 bytes each at 128 kbit/s); `/api/status` `listen` shows `devices` (`version`, `set`, `sample_rate`, `sending`), `selects`, `frames` and `refused`.
- **The sound itself:** decode the captured packets with Chromium's WebCodecs `AudioDecoder` (`opus`, 48 kHz stereo) on a routed https page and read peak and RMS: music reads like music (−12.8 dBFS peak, −25.9 dBFS RMS on the backing tracks); a wrong byte order would read as full-scale noise.
- **CPU:** dasio's sampler on the band Live's process, 7.5 s samples, A/B windows of the same song from the same spot, after Live has run a while (the first window after a restart reads ~140 % high from warm-up). One streaming device measured 432 % → 433 % (no measurable cost).
- **The racks off while idle (#238):** with every `Listen` rack off and nobody listening, dasio's callbacks over the 2.67 ms period as in #238's OFF window (28 in 10 minutes, no missed or late block, transport stopped); a listen streams within about 2 s of the tap (the device comes up, announces, polls) and its rack reads Device On 0 again within 1 s of the stop (the hub's `listen` `rack` records, `on` true then false, `result` done); read `latency_in_samples` of the track's devices with the rack off and on. **SimLive's off device is the design's model, not yet a PC fact:** with every rack off, read `/api/status` `listen.devices` and `selects` over a few seconds and record on #238 whether a device in a rack that is off still announces or polls (Max's scheduler may run on while the device's audio is off); SimLive then follows what the PC showed (`live-script.md`). After any set change, save the sets with every `Listen` rack off (the save proven by the file, `deploy-pc.md` step (1) of the Live restart; `live-script.md`: a LOM change does not mark the set modified).
- **The devices' threads and identity (#240), proved only on the PC** (CI checks the structure, nothing runs the patches): (1) a renamed track or rack reaches `/api/status` `listen.devices` (and the LUFS and meter keys) within a second, with no read each second (`live.observer` on a parent's `name` gives the name when its id is set and again at each rename); (2) a device moved to another track names the new track (`live.path`'s middle outlet gives the new parent's id for `this_device canonical_parent…`, and gives it at the first `path` message too); (3) a parent path that holds nothing reads `-`; (4) the select still starts and ends a stream (`qmetro 250`, the hold through `deferlow`); (5) dasio's callbacks over the period, A/B per device type.
- **A check restores what it changed:** a check that switches a rack on by hand reads its Device On first and puts it back; the sets are never saved with a rack on.
