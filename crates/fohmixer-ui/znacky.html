TAGS IN THE TUNER

What fohmixer shows is set by a Tuner on a track in Ableton. Add one to the track (best switched off: it changes no sound and costs nothing) and rename it (Ctrl+R). The track's own name is not used.

Example: "Vox 1" +G:VOCALS:2 +G:TALKSHOW:1 +PIN +MG

SYNTAX

"Vox 1"name on the fader, in quotes, may have several words
+G:VOCALSgroup: capital letters, digits, - and _ (_ shows as a space); the fader sits in Live's track order
+G:VOCALS:2group and position in it (1 to 999); may repeat: one fader in several groups
+PINalways on screen: in every view and when shifting ◀ ▶
+MGmute guard: a change needs a second tap
+ID:VOX1strip tag for a linked mute (below): capital letters, digits, - and _
+MUTE:VOX1this track mutes and unmutes together with the strip that has +ID:VOX1
+ID:REVstrip tag for a linked fader too (below): the same +ID can serve the mute and the fader
+FADER:REVthis track's fader moves by the same dB when you move the strip with +ID:REV (if the link is on)
+REVERBreverb fader: the REVERB button lowers it by 10 dB and returns it; REVERB AUTO does it when Live stops. Only on a strip (quoted name and group); without its own name (or with a name another strip has) it is not a reverb fader.
+AUTOTUNEthis track's autotune (below): the rack named Latencies, also inside another rack, and its first macro; on = 127, off = 0 (stream)
+AUTOTUNE:0the same, on = 0 and off = 127 (repro)
+LUFS:-25loudness of a mic after its Trim IN, judged against the target (here −25 LUFS): LUFS IN (below)
+LUFSloudness of an output (the PA return, a bus), plain: LUFS OUT (below)

Write tags in capital letters (as in AbleSet). A Tuner without quotes and without a + tag is an ordinary Tuner and fohmixer ignores it.

LINKED MUTE

One MUTE can mute several tracks at once, for example the repro and the stream of the same singer. The strip's Tuner gets +ID: with a short name you choose, and the Tuner of every other track or return gets +MUTE: with the same name. A Tuner with only +MUTE (no quoted name) makes no strip in fohmixer. If the track already has a Tuner with tags, add +MUTE to it (two Tuners on one track is an error).

Example: the singer's Tuner "Vox 1" +G:STAGE +ID:VOX1, the Tuner of their stream track +MUTE:VOX1.

The button shows a chain under the name with the number of linked tracks: grey when all play, red when all are muted, and orange, for example 1/3, when someone muted only some of them by hand in Ableton. A tap mutes all; when all are muted, a tap unmutes all. The strip goes dark only when all are muted, and then the chain is red without its number and the word MUTED is at the right of the line under the name. A name is valid within one Live set.

LINKED FADERS

A strip's fader can lead the faders of other tracks or returns, like a linked mute. The leading strip's Tuner has +ID: with a short name and every partner's Tuner has +FADER: with the same name. While the link is on, a drag or double-tap on the leader moves every partner by the same dB. When you move a partner by itself, only it moves. A Tuner with only +FADER and +MUTE (no quoted name) makes no strip in fohmixer.

Example: the repro reverb's Tuner "Rev PA" +G:EFFECTS +REVERB +ID:REV, the stream reverb's Tuner "Rev ST" +G:EFFECTS +REVERB +FADER:REV.

The link is switched on and off in the leader's detail (hold SEL at the strip's bottom left): LINKED TO …. While it is on, the leader shows a dB mark with a chain under its name and a teal line on the knob; the partner shows the same mark outlined. Beside two other marks the mark is the chain alone, and on a narrow strip with ! and a mute chain it is not shown. A partner's detail says who leads it.

Podklady All (the backing tracks) has no track of its own: its tracks are the tracks whose Tuner has +FADER:PODKLADY. It is always linked.

AUTOTUNE

The AUTOTUNE button shows and controls the singers' autotune. The Tuner of every track with autotune gets +AUTOTUNE (on when the rack's first macro is at 127) or +AUTOTUNE:0 (on at 0). Fohmixer finds the rack named Latencies in the track, also inside another rack (at most three racks deep), and switches its first macro. There must be exactly one such rack in the track. A Tuner with only +MUTE, +FADER and +AUTOTUNE (no quoted name) makes no strip, so a stream track can have +MUTE:VOX1 +AUTOTUNE. The tags count only when the layout has an AUTOTUNE button.

Example: the singer's Tuner "Vox 1" +G:STAGE +ID:VOX1 +AUTOTUNE:0, the Tuner of their stream track +MUTE:VOX1 +AUTOTUNE.

Every singer is a pair of tracks: their PA track (the Tuner with +ID:) and their stream track (the Tuner with +MUTE: with the same name). The key is always written from the song's locator and the Key {G} clips on the Sections track.

AUTO (hold AUTOTUNE for 1 s and release; the same switches it off): while it is on, every singer tunes when Live plays in a song with a key and their key is written and read back; otherwise they are on their DRY chain. With AUTO off everyone is on DRY. The button's second line shows the state and the key: off · F, stopped · F, no song, no key, checking · F, tuning · F, waiting · F (nobody tunes and the reason is in the panel), tuning · F · 1 off, tuning · F · 1 !.

The button lights orange when every singer tunes, is dark when nobody tunes, and has an orange rim when only some tune. A yellow ! at the top left means a singer whose key or track is not right, or an error in the song locators.

The singers' panel: a tap on AUTOTUNE opens it beside the middle strip; a tap outside the panel (that tap does nothing else) or on AUTOTUNE again closes it. A singer's row shows their stream and PA (TUNE, DRY, — with no track), the key (F ✓ written and read back, F … still being checked, ! not right, the reason under the row) and their OFF/ON switch. When the hub itself keeps a singer on DRY (its fall back to DRY is not kept, it waits for the track list or for the rack to be found again), the key still shows and only that reason is under the row. At the top are the song and the key, and the next key change with its clip; at the bottom the counts from the locators and their errors.

The yellow ! in the AUTOTUNE button's corner flashes briefly when Live's track list changes or after a reconnect, while the hub checks the racks again; that is no error.

Procedure: switch one singer off

  1. Tap AUTOTUNE: the singers' panel opens.
  2. Tap OFF by the singer: their stream and PA go to DRY at once and stay there through the next songs and a hub restart.
  3. A tap on ON switches them back on; they tune by the rules again.

LUFS

A strip with +LUFS shows its short-term loudness (as Sonible trueLevel's short-term: 3 s, one decimal) as a small number at the top of the column left of the fader, under the caption LUFS over IN or OUT (on a narrow phone strip the number alone, upright). The number comes from the Max for Live device FohMixer LUFS on that track; it has no controls and passes no sound, so it always sits in a parallel chain of an Audio Effect Rack: on a mic in CORE Effects beside trueLevel, on an output point in a rack of its own (an empty chain plus the device's chain).

−25.4 green+LUFS:-25: within 1 LU of the target (−26.0 to −24.0), as trueLevel's green band
−21.3 greyfurther from the target, any direction
−18.3 grey+LUFS: the output's short-term after its fader, never coloured
−∞ greysilence (−70 or less: nobody sings), the caption stays
(nothing)no reading yet, the device stopped, or Live offline; the caption goes too

The number is quieter than Live's dB on purpose. An armed mic also needs the FohMixer Meter device last in its chain (in a rack of its own, as above).

VIEWS

A group the main view does not have is shown by a button in the middle strip (VIEWS): its faders and all pinned ones. Another tap takes you back.

ERRORS

  • CONFLICT: two Tuners with the same name. Both faders are off until you change one name.
  • Yellow ! under the name (TAG?): unknown tag, bad value, two Tuners on one track or two faders in one position. The fader works; fix the Tuner's name.
  • Linked mute: the same +ID on two strips (both get a yellow ! and neither links), +MUTE with a name no strip has, or +MUTE with the name of its own strip. The number on the chain is the count of tracks the MUTE mutes, the strip itself included. Check the chain: if it is missing, or its number is (after a moment) smaller than the number of tracks you linked, some +MUTE Tuner has a typo.
  • Linked fader: the same +ID on two strips (nothing links), +FADER with a name no strip has, +FADER with the name of its own strip, or the same +FADER twice. Podklady All with no +FADER:PODKLADY at all is red.
  • Autotune: +AUTOTUNE: with a value other than 0, or a second +AUTOTUNE in one Tuner, is a bad tag. A track without a Latencies rack, or with two, is not switched: the AUTOTUNE button shows a yellow ! at the top left (the other tracks still switch), and when no rack is found at all it is red. The hub status (/api/status, autotune.targets) says which track and why.
  • LUFS: +LUFS: with a target outside −40 to −10, or two +LUFS tags, is a bad LUFS tag. A strip with +LUFS but no FohMixer LUFS device on its track (no LUFS meter), or with two (two LUFS meters, also when both Live sets have one at that place), shows a yellow ! under its name and no number; an armed +LUFS: mic without FohMixer Meter is no meter device. The hub status (/api/status, lufs.strips) says which strip and why.
  • A Tuner without a group (+G:) is shown nowhere.

A change in Ableton shows on the iPad within a second.
